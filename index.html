Spooky Halls

Alpha 0.5: Boss Fight Update

Four things woke up in the corner rooms. Something worse sleeps in the middle.

Kill all four to wake it. Then finish it. You have a knife. Chests hold guns. Five levels, each harder, with stronger guns later.

WASD / left stick to move. Hold Shift or Run to sprint (it drains stamina). M mutes sound. Mouse or Attack to strike (Space works too). Tap a slot, scroll or press 1-5 to switch weapons (Q cycles). Med kits heal you fully. An ammo crate drops every 30 seconds. Gunshots draw monsters.

Pick a level. Beat one to unlock the next.

Find the armor

Somewhere in these halls there is a piece of armor. It is important. It raises your max HP from 100 to 150, and you will need it.
